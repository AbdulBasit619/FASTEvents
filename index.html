RegistrationPosted Nov 5

Tech Fest registrations are open

Teams of up to four can register for any of the 30 competitions until December 1. Each team needs a faculty-approved lead. Registration is free for FAST students; guests pay a small fee at the desk.

Questions? Visit the Events Office, Block B, Room 12.

SchedulePosted Nov 7

Cricket Cup fixtures released

Eight departments are drawn into two groups. Group matches start December 3 at the Main Ground, with the final on December 10. Captains must confirm squads 48 hours before their first match.

SponsorsPosted Nov 8

Career Fair adds 12 new companies

Twelve more employers across software, banking and telecom will join the Career Fair. Carry printed CVs and register for interview slots at the Career Services desk.
